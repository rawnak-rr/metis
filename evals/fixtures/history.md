# European Integration

The Treaty of Paris was signed in 1951 and established the European Coal and Steel Community. It pooled coal and steel production among six founding states.

The Treaties of Rome were signed in 1957. They created the European Economic Community and the European Atomic Energy Community.

The Schuman Declaration of 9 May 1950 proposed placing French and West German coal and steel production under a common authority. The proposal helped initiate the integration process.

The Maastricht Treaty was signed in 1992 and created the European Union, while also setting a path toward economic and monetary union.
