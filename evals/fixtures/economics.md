# Introductory Economics

Opportunity cost is the value of the best alternative forgone when a choice is made. It is not necessarily equal to the money paid.

Price elasticity of demand is the percentage change in quantity demanded divided by the percentage change in price. Demand is elastic when the absolute value of this ratio is greater than one.

A binding price ceiling is set below the market-clearing price and can create a shortage because quantity demanded exceeds quantity supplied.

Comparative advantage depends on lower opportunity cost, not absolute productivity. Specialization according to comparative advantage can create gains from trade.
