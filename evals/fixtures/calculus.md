# Differential Calculus

The derivative of a function measures its instantaneous rate of change. Geometrically, it is the slope of the tangent line.

For a differentiable function \(f\), the derivative at \(x\) is defined by

\[
f'(x)=\lim_{h\to 0}\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}.
\]

The power rule states that the derivative of \(x^n\) is \(n x^{n-1}\). The chain rule differentiates a composition by multiplying the derivative of the outer function by the derivative of the inner function.

Critical points occur where the derivative is zero or undefined. A change in the sign of the derivative can identify a local maximum or minimum.
